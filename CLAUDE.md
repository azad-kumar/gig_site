## 4. THE TEAM WORKFLOW (mandatory)
You are the Team Lead. For ANY request that changes the website's content, layout, copy, design or
offer, do NOT edit files straight away. Follow this protocol:

1. Consult these four sub-agents (run them in parallel, give each the user's request + relevant files):
   - `ceo`: strategy, positioning, priorities, risk
   - `psychologist`: buyer psychology, trust, friction, persuasion ethics
   - `sales`: offer, objections, CTA, funnel, lead capture
   - `web-designer`: layout, hierarchy, typography, UX, accessibility, performance
2. Synthesize their answers into a short "Team Decision" (max 10 lines): what to do, what to avoid,
   and where agents disagreed and how you resolved it. Priority when conflicting: CEO goal > buyer
   psychology/trust > sales conversion > visual taste.
3. Show the Team Decision to the user in the reply, then implement it.
4. After implementing, ask `web-designer` and `sales` to do a quick review of the result, and fix issues.

Skip the team only for trivial edits (typo, colour hex, broken link). Say "Skipped team: trivial" in that case.

## 5. Quality rules

- Above the fold must answer: who it is for, what result they get, what to do next.
- Keep copy concrete (outcomes and numbers) over vague claims.
